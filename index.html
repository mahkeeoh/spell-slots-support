Support for the iPhone and iPad app

Need a hand, found a bug, or have an idea?

Email habituateco@gmail.com. Please include your iOS version, your device, and what you were doing when the problem happened. I read every message.

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. If you bought the upgrade in version 1.x, you already have Pro.

What do I get with Pro?

Pro is a single purchase, not a subscription, and it works with Family Sharing. It adds unlimited characters, a concentration tracker, class feature tracking, pinned spells and notes, a casting-time filter, iCloud sync, a Home Screen widget and the homebrew spell editor. Free users track one character with every spell and the full slot tracker.

Where did my characters from version 1.x go?

They move over automatically when you update. After updating, open each character and check its level and subclass under Edit.

Which rules does the app follow?

You choose the 2014 or 2024 rules for each character. Slots, spells known or prepared, and cantrips follow the rules you chose.

A spell or a number looks wrong. What should I do?

Email me the spell name, the character's class, level and rule set, and what you expected to see. Corrections ship in app updates.

How does iCloud sync work?

Pro can sync your characters between your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud. I cannot see your data. Sign in to iCloud on each device and turn on iCloud in the app's Settings.

The Home Screen widget is not updating.

Open the app once, then remove and re-add the widget. Taps on the widget are applied the next time the app opens.

Does the app collect my data?

No. There are no ads, no account and no tracking. See the privacy policy.
